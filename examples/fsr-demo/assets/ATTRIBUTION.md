Group [glTF-Sample-Assets](https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets)
repository. They are bundled with `fsr-demo` solely as demonstration content.

## Notes

- **Damaged Helmet** carries a dual notice: the current glTF rebuild by ctxwing
  is CC BY 4.0, while the earlier model it derives from (theblueturtle_) was
  released under the non-commercial CC BY-NC 4.0. Treat the asset as
  non-commercial-only out of caution.
- The full, authoritative license text for each model lives in
  [`LICENSES/<ModelName>.LICENSE.md`](LICENSES/), copied unmodified from the
  upstream repository.
